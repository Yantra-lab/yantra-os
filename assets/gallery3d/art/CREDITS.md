# Night Gallery: the six paintings

All six are public-domain works; the scans are the museums' open-access images. Stored upright,
cropped to 4:5 (1024×1280); the app flips them for the 3D canvas itself.
The other two paintings in the hall are not images: they are drawn live by code (`app.nim`).
