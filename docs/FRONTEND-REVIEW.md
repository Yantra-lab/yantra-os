## What visibly improved — preserve these choices

- **Aura is now a useful empty space.** Search, app launch, new tab, recents/empty-state copy, window counts and shortcuts replace the release's giant clock over an otherwise unused purple field. The primary action is unmistakable.
- **The hierarchy is coherent.** The large but short headline, central search, quick actions, then tool cards scan in that order. Periwinkle focus/action color and the restrained wallpaper connect the home to the dock instead of fighting them.
- **The theme has a real base.** Semantic M3 roles and shared type choices in [config.nim:12–55](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/config.nim#L12-L55) are better than inventing a palette per widget. Keep the low-chroma surfaces and warm accent used for date/space metadata.
- **The gallery now shows the art.** The supplied Vermeer plate replaces the release's empty central frame. Moving from near-black walls to warm paper makes the painting, text, and attribution readable together.
- **Editorial restraint works.** The serif title, small ochre index, thin rules and matte frame support the art. The numbered contact sheet exposes all eight choices and makes the selected work, next title and previous/next order understandable without relying on the old decorative room diagram.

## Visible problems and exact changes

1. **P0 — the gallery crops different paintings into one portrait template.** The main plate is fixed at 352×440 with `fit = 1`, and every thumbnail is 64×80 with the same setting ([app.nim:114–120](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L114-L120), [149–162](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L149-L162)); `fit = 1` is cover. Landscape works in the contact sheet are visibly cut into portrait slices. Change both to contain (`fit = 0`), retain a neutral matte behind the unused area, and size the main frame from asset aspect ratio within a bounded region rather than cropping Wave, Wheat Field or Water Lilies to Vermeer's shape.
2. **P1 — the gallery is a fixed desktop composition, not an integrated window layout.** Root width/height are 1280×816; details, controls and contact sheet use absolute left/top coordinates ([app.nim:99–149](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L99-L149)). A narrower desktop tile will clip rather than reflow. Replace this with one vertical header/body/contact-sheet flow: a two-column body above a wrapping strip, stacking details below the artwork once width is constrained. Put Previous/Next in normal flow immediately after the metadata; their current absolute `top = 574px` creates a large dead gap under short records.
3. **P1 — contact-sheet affordances are too small for their job.** Only the 72×20 number is the button; the image and title are separate non-clickable nodes ([app.nim:163–174](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L163-L174)). Make the entire thumbnail card one button with a 32–36px minimum number row, keep the selected outline, and truncate only after allowing two title lines. On the supplied capture the 9px captions and uppercase helper labels are needlessly faint.
4. **P1 — Aura reading copy is consistently undersized.** Tool notes are 11px, shortcut labels 11px, shortcut keys 10px, and several useful counters/labels 9px ([ui.nim:674–684](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L674-L684), [747–788](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L747-L788)). At the supplied size these recede into the wallpaper. Use 12–13px for reading/action copy and key hints; reserve 9–10px mono for nonessential overlines. Raise low-content card borders from alpha `0.07–0.09` to a visible shared outline token rather than adding more shadows.
5. **P1 — the home repeats the same small catalog three ways.** `WideSidebar` renders `catalog` as QUICK ACCESS, `HomePage` renders it as YOUR TOOLS, and New Tab renders those same cards again ([ui.nim:329–334](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L329-L334), [747–752](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L747-L752), [790–806](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L790-L806)). Hide QUICK ACCESS while the empty Home owns the catalog; leave sidebar space for actual tabs. On New Tab, prioritize recents before the full catalog so it is not simply Home inside a smaller tile.
6. **P2 — two clocks compete on an otherwise quiet home.** `HomePage` shows a 58px local clock while `Dock/BarClock` simultaneously splits the same time into vertical hour/minute digits ([ui.nim:420–434](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L420-L434), [762–775](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L762-L775)). Hide the dock clock only while this Home card is present; retain it for windowed spaces. Let the existing dock spacer absorb that space; do not add another decoration.
7. **P2 — the empty-state content ends too early.** At 1280×800, three tool cards and the two right cards occupy the upper half while the `row(grow = 1)` reserves a largely empty lower field ([ui.nim:722–789](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L722-L789)). Let tool cards grow to the available column width and use a compact two-column recents/shortcuts area below them when recents exist. Do not add another hero or ornamental panel to fill it.
8. **P2 — distinguish adjacent utility icons.** Calculator and Notes collapse into similar murky olive tiles in both sidebar and tool cards. Keep `Tonal`, but assign visibly different tint pairs at the catalog source and verify the 26px sidebar rendering, not only the 36px card icon.
9. **Open-state readability remains unresolved.** In the additional `desktop-open-v3.png`, the New Tab title, field, cards and background clock are all extremely faint. Pixels alone do not establish whether this is a transition capture or an overlay/compositing bug. Do not use it as settled proof; reproduce after animation settles, inspect the real painted buffer, and only then change opacity or overlay code.

## Lead follow-up after the independent review

- **The dim window was a stale capture harness, not an engine/app opacity defect.** Its loop repeatedly called `tick(16)` even though the engine expects an absolute monotonic time. Numeric probes found ancestor opacity 0.20180619 at 16 ms versus 1.0 at 640 ms. The rebuilt current harness advances `16 × frameNumber`, and the shipped `proofs/desktop-open.png` is now bright. No engine/app motion was changed to achieve that.
- **The home no longer renders the entire catalog in three places.** The sidebar offers the featured trio and an All applications path; the Library owns the complete catalog and its whole card launches on click.
- **The unused lower area now carries a quiet cyan/champagne horizon** rather than more inventory cards. It is the one signature atmospheric treatment, not another control.
- The gallery's installed-path and aspect-fit follow-up is integrated in `6fff2508`; its six external JPEGs resolve from `apps/gallery/art`, not from a harness-only asset map.

## Source-language friction worth removing

- **Selection duplicates rendering.** Gallery lines 153–174 repeat panel/canvas/image/button/text for selected and unselected states. A `selected` style/state on one contact-card component would keep one artwork path and change only border, fill and weight.
- **Collection cardinality is written three times.** `" / 08"`, `"EIGHT WORKS"`, and `0 ..< 8` duplicate `works.len` ([app.nim:54](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L54), [110–111](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L110-L111), [149–150](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L149-L150)). Derive all three; iteration and position formatting should work for an empty or changed collection without another template edit.
- **Stringly typed art variants complicate a simple either/or.** External versus generated work is represented by empty/non-empty `art` and `gen` strings (`"flow"`/`"vib"`), and every stack paints both Canvas and Image even when one source is empty ([app.nim:4–39](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L4-L39), [94–97](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L94-L97)). A DSL-level tagged image/canvas content variant would remove sentinel strings and empty loads.
- **Canvas command assembly is boilerplate-heavy.** `iv` plus repeated `result.add " M/L/S …"`, `sin/cos` coordinate mutation and packed color strings obscure the drawing ([app.nim:52–97](/tmp/wt-gallery-craft/yantra-os/apps/gallery/app.nim#L52-L97)). Typed `moveTo/lineTo/stroke` path builders and numeric colors would preserve the exact emitted commands while removing concatenation and conversion helpers.
- **Magic layout numbers leak across both dialects.** Gallery uses `alignItems = 1/2`, `align = 1/2`, `fit = 1`; desktop mixes named `center/stretch` with numeric `1/2/4` ([ui.nim:589–590](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L589-L590), [927–944](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L927-L944)). Use one shared enum vocabulary (`start/center/end/stretch`, `contain/cover`) and one px policy instead of requiring authors to memorize renderer integers.
- **Desktop callbacks and state access obscure simple controls.** Optional buttons require repeated `proc (): bool = false` and `proc () = …`; reactive conditions are peppered with `.get/.set`. Static-or-signal boolean arguments, event assignment sugar and `bind` for inputs/switches would remove this without changing behavior.
- **Actionable rows rebuild button behavior by hand.** Home cards, quick actions, jewels and manage-space are generic row/column nodes with hover plus `onClick` ([ui.nim:674–699](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L674-L699), [776–780](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L776-L780)). A shared action/card primitive should supply pressed, focus, disabled and keyboard semantics once; then pointer-only tests are not the whole accessibility contract.
- **Aura's “single source” palette is already bypassed.** `filletSksl` hard-codes the surface RGB as floats ([ui.nim:5–16](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L5-L16)) although `m3surface` lives in config. Generate shader constants from that role; a theme change must not leave dark drawer corners behind.
- **Do not add a third syntax.** Gallery's `view` with capitalized `Column/Image` nodes and desktop's included component/lower-case node form need the same fit, alignment, action and binding semantics before further sugar is introduced.

## Highest-priority integration tests

1. **Extend `t_desk_render` with final-state assertions.** Starting at Home, activate Calculator and separately New Tab: assert the focused address, the correct app/start content, Home disappearance, one intended tile, and the sidebar count. Paint only after enter/exit motion settles and inspect both frames. Different hashes alone, or boxes larger than zero, are insufficient.
2. **Reproduce the faint New Tab state.** Assert foreground visibility after the launcher is closed and after window focus changes. Include opacity/compositing checks for the title, search field, primary action and desk clock so a fully laid-out but nearly invisible frame cannot pass.
3. **Exercise every gallery work.** Click each numbered contact card and assert its title, `WORK n / total`, metadata and next title; assert Previous from first wraps to last and Next from last wraps to first. Verify all six external images load non-empty content and both generated works issue their canvas drawing without an empty image request.
4. **Golden-test art fitting.** Cover portrait Vermeer/Ravi Varma and landscape Wave/Wheat Field/Monet at both plate and thumbnail sizes. Assert contain preserves the complete source aspect ratio and that matte bars, not cropping, absorb the difference.
5. **Test shared search as a focus/state problem.** AddressBar, Home, Start, Library and Launcher share `query`, selection and submit behavior ([ui.nim:50–80](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L50-L80), [176–184](/tmp/wt-frontend/apps/desktop/ui.nim#L176-L184)). Type, Super+D, type, Enter and Escape: exactly one result may launch, focus must have one owner, and closing must restore the intended draft/focus rather than another visible input's state.
6. **Drive without a pointer.** Tab through tool cards, quick actions, manage-space, contact cards and dock controls; activate with Enter/Space and show a focus indicator. This specifically tests the generic clickable rows that `t_desk_render` currently proves only geometrically.
7. **Lay out at integration sizes.** Test desktop and gallery at the real test viewport 1100×768 as well as their supplied captures, with wide/slim sidebar, long recent names and every gallery record. Assert controls remain inside their clipping parent, the contact sheet stays reachable, and no content depends on the original 1280px absolute canvas.
8. **Theme and typography regression.** Toggle glass and change surface/accent roles in a fixture; drawer fillet pixels must match their parent and focused/unfocused borders must remain distinguishable. Assert the actual Noto/DejaVu faces load, including the Devanagari brand and long gallery titles, rather than silently accepting fallback metrics.
